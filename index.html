Graphics

Subtle risograph grain over flat colour. Use the transparent texture on top of artwork and backgrounds.

Visual style

One bold idea per piece. Flat colour, real white space, deadpan characters, a little mischief. Printed texture, never glossy.

Voice

Bittersweet, with a wink.

  • Dry, warm and a little cheeky. Never mean, never crude.
  • One idea per piece. If it needs explaining, it isn't finished.
  • Original designs only. No borrowed characters, brands or logos.
